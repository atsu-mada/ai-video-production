Shared Continuity Rules

Use the smallest set of references that can control the shot. Give each reference one primary role: identity, environment geometry, object construction, object state, storyboard blocking, or text and camera direction. Record both the attributes it contributes and those it must not contribute.

Identity and geometry

  • Keep one canonical identity, object silhouette, environment plan, material signature, and lighting logic across every compatible state.
  • Put materially different states on separate references instead of combining mutually exclusive states on one board. Keep reusable construction and scene-specific visible state separate.
  • Preserve scale, attachment points, openings, seams, and component positions when a reference is reused.

Staging and orientation

  • Describe one visible subject action and one camera action per cut unless a deliberate compound move is part of the approved beat.
  • Stage actors toward actual scene partners and objects. Screens, photographs, signs, and mirrors retain a plausible real or reflected axis.
  • Let the camera observe from the side or over the shoulder when the subject is reading or interacting; avoid unexplained direct address.

Text and effects

  • Keep production captions, labels, logos, watermarks, and storyboard marks out of generated footage. Allow story-required in-world writing and ordinary interface copy only from an approved state reference.
  • Treat a storyboard as a blocking reference. It does not become a compositing layer or a final-frame overlay.
  • Describe VFX geometry, material, optical behavior, and state transitions separately from storyboard marks.

Spatial anchor continuity

A large physical anchor (a planet, a building, a vehicle, a landmark) is one object in one place for the whole continuous shot or sequence.

  • Camera motion changes where the anchor sits on screen; the anchor itself does not move, duplicate, or reappear from another direction.
  • Before a move, write where the anchor is relative to the camera and subject (for example "below and ahead of the craft"), and after the move, where it must therefore appear on screen.
  • If the anchor leaves frame, it returns only from the side the camera path implies. Never re-introduce it as a new entrance from elsewhere.
  • In previz and prompts, state "the same <anchor> stays in its original position; only the camera moves" and check the first and last frames of each job against that plan.

参照の可視範囲と除外属性

ショットで実際に見える身体部位、視線の相手、焦点対象を先に決める。POVで人物の身体が見えないなら、人物の全身参照を機械的に追加しない。顔の寄りには顔、身体断片には該当部位など、必要な同一性だけを渡す。参照ごとに継承する形・材質・位置と、継承しない画角・背景・状態を記録する。

カメラ経路の全域を同じ環境設計で支え、途中で見えるランドマークと接続点を確認する。競合が起きたら、観測と原因仮説を分けた比較を行う。
