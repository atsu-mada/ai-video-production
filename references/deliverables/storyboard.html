Image-only Storyboard

Create an image-only blocking sheet on pure white. Each picture panel is 16:9 and uses clean black contour lines without tonal shading. Each panel answers where the subject is, its direction and relative scale, and one principal visible action. Show several ordered panels when they are time samples of one continuous cut; express change through distinct poses and compositions, never arrows, afterimages, speed lines, or duplicated ghost poses.

Keep enough facial and anatomical structure to recognize the intended subject, while minimizing garment folds, decorative texture, and incidental detail. Reduce environments to a few landmark lines and objects to the outer contour unless a functional detail is the action. Exclude shading, gray, color, texture, production text, digits, timecodes, lens or camera names, pseudo-writing, logos, watermarks, arrows, flash bursts, and motion trails. Put readable paper or screen content in a separate state reference rather than the storyboard.
