Delivery Variants and QA

Plan platform deliverables from one accepted master edit. A variant changes framing, subtitle placement, and possibly total duration; it never silently changes the edit's content. Record every variant as its own versioned file.

Aspect-ratio variants with designed bands

When the master is 16:9 and a platform wants 1:1 or 9:16, prefer keeping the full 16:9 picture and filling the remaining area with designed bands over cropping into the action.

  • Scale the 16:9 picture to the full frame width and center it (or place it slightly above center for 9:16 so the lower band can hold text).
  • Fill the bands with a design that belongs to the film: a matching texture, a blurred and darkened extension of the same frame, or a flat color sampled from the grade. Avoid unrelated stock patterns.
  • Put subtitles and telop inside a band, not over the picture, and keep them inside the platform's UI safe area (see below). Keep the same font, size ratio, and timing across variants.
  • Crop into the picture only when a cut has a single centered subject and the crop is reviewed per cut.

Platform UI safe areas

Vertical feeds overlay the account name, caption, and action buttons on the video. As a planning rule, keep text out of roughly the top 12%, the bottom 20–25%, and the right 12–15% of a 9:16 frame, and out of the bottom 15% of a 1:1 frame. Check a real preview of each platform before final export; overlays change between app versions.

Delivery QA checklist

  1. ffprobe every export: container duration, video duration, and audio duration agree within one frame; resolution, frame rate, and pixel format match the variant.
  2. Run blackdetect, freezedetect, and silencedetect; every hit is either intended or fixed.
  3. Loudness: measure with ebur128 and keep true peak at or below −1 dBTP (apply a limiter if needed).
  4. Inspect frames about 0.3 s either side of every cut for flashes, duplicated frames, or mismatched grades.
  5. Check subtitle and telop placement per variant against the safe areas.
  6. Confirm the total duration fits the platform cap for the target account.
  7. If the edit was built by concatenating segments, confirm there is no audio drift (see concat audio drift).
  8. Play each variant end to end in real time before calling it approved; technical checks do not replace viewing.

Version bumping

  • Every new output gets a new version suffix, for example <title-slug>_<variant>_v<NN>.mp4. Never overwrite an accepted or delivered version.
  • A fix to one variant bumps that variant only; a fix to the master bumps the master and every variant rebuilt from it.
  • Record which master version each variant was built from.
