Character Sheet

Create one minimal identity board for one recurring human or creature. Use a uniform #808080 background and exactly three zones: front-facing neck-down body, matching back-facing neck-down body, and one large neutral three-quarter head-and-shoulders portrait. The portrait is the only visible face.

Keep flat informative light, deep focus, anatomy or wardrobe locks, and a neutral stance. Exclude props, scenery, text, grids, swatches, extra expressions, and unrelated identities. Give wet, injured, contaminated, transformed, or alternate-costume states their own boards.
